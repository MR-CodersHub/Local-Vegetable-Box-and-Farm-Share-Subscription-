How We Grow

Farming Practices We Require

No Synthetic Pesticides

Weekly residue testing. Any failed lot is rejected and composted — farmers are still paid.

Soil-First Rotation

Cover crops and rest seasons rebuild soil. That’s why corn rests this month — health over yield.

Fair-Share Pricing

Farmers set floor prices each season. 92 paise of every rupee of produce value reaches the farm.

Zero-Plastic Boxes

Compostable liners, returnable crates and paper bands. 38,000 plastic pouches avoided in 2026.
