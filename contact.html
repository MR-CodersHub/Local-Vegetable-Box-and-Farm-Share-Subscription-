Quick Answers

Common Questions

What are your delivery hours? +
Subscriber boxes ride Sunday & Tuesday morning (7–11 AM) and evening (4–8 PM) slots. Trial-box and extras orders placed before Thursday 10 PM join the weekend harvest route.
How do I track my delivery? +
Once your order is dispatched from our hub, you will receive an SMS and WhatsApp message with a live GPS tracking link.
How does your freshness return work? +
If any item is damaged or not fresh, take a photo and send it via WhatsApp to +1 800 373 4765 within 24 hours for a full refund or free replacement.
