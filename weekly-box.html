Box FAQ

How Customization Works

Can I swap items I don’t like?
Yes — remove anything and add replacements from the swap shelf at no extra cost (up to your plan’s item count). Extras beyond that are billed à la carte.
When is the cutoff?
Thursday 10 PM for Sunday–Tuesday deliveries. The timer at the top counts down live. After cutoff, boxes are locked for harvest packing.
What if I’m travelling?
Skip a week or pause from your subscriber dashboard in one tap — no calls, no fees. Resume whenever you’re back.
