Our Journey

How We Started a Fresh Movement

HarvestBox began with a simple observation: supermarket produce took days to reach shelves, losing nutrients and flavor along the way, while local organic farmers struggled to find direct markets.

We bridged this gap with a weekly farm-share box subscribers customize, pause and track. Today, we pack 1,200+ boxes a week for 12,000+ subscriber households while supporting 50+ regional organic farm families.

2018 — The Beginning
Started with 3 Partner Farms in Ooty

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Purity First

We never compromise on organic integrity. Zero synthetic pesticides, chemical ripening, or wax coatings on any produce we deliver.

Fair Farmer Prices

Farmers receive 70% of retail price — far above standard market rates. When you shop with us, you directly sustain local agricultural communities.

Zero Plastic Waste

100% plastic-free packaging. All deliveries use reusable jute bags and compostable paper wraps to protect our planet.
